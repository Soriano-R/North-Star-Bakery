Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small neighborhood shop built around a simple idea: good baking should bring people together. We still prepare our products in small batches and welcome regulars and first-time visitors the same way.

How We Choose Ingredients

We focus on dependable ingredients and work with regional suppliers when possible. Seasonal fruit and rotating flavors help us keep the menu fresh throughout the year.

Meet the Team

Our bakers arrive early to mix dough, shape loaves, and prepare the pastry case. Our front counter team helps customers choose everyday favorites and plan orders for special occasions.
